What these numbers mean

A seeded Monte Carlo simulation samples independent triangular task durations from your three estimates. It schedules one student’s work, pauses at the daily hour limit, and respects release days and dependencies.

The percentage is the fraction of simulated weeks that met every deadline. It is conditional on the inputs and this scheduling policy, not an empirically calibrated prediction. A scope cut assumes proportional duration savings without reducing quality; decide whether that tradeoff is realistic.

What it leaves out

Shared bad days, fatigue, task-switching costs, interruptions and changing priorities are not modeled. A heuristic chooses the most urgent downstream deadline; it does not prove an optimal schedule. “Impossible” warnings below are limited to structural constraints.

Everything stays in this browser. No account, AI inference, calendar access or network API. Export a replay to reproduce every result.
