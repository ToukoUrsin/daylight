## Title

Daylight: simulate your student week, then change one thing (InfinityX 2K26)

## Description

A plan is a guess. Daylight is a student workload planner that gives each task a best, likely and worst duration. It plays out 1,600 possible weeks inside the hours you actually have, then compares one change on the same random draws.

In the synthetic example, 13.5 likely hours fit inside 17 available hours. Every deadline is still met in only 51% of simulated weeks. Cutting the essay's scope by 30% raises that to 94%. These are simulation estimates from authored inputs, not predictions or guarantees.
